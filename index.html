Biography

I am Harry Huang (Wenyuan Huang, 黄问远), a senior majoring in Math and Computer Science at the University of Wisconsin-Madison. My research interests include various topics about Large Language Models, specifically agents, continual learning, and LLM reasoning, also including a strong passion for Operating Systems and Machine Learning Systems.

I'm an active competitive programming participant (ICPC and OI). I won three NOIP (CSP-S) First Prizes prior to college and was the champion of the 2024 ICPC NCNA (see school news). Currently, I am serving as a co-coach of the UW-Madison ICPC team for the 2025-2026 season (see school news).

Outside of academics, I enjoy sports (badminton, swimming, ice skating, bouldering) and music. I started playing piano at 4 and began learning the cello in college. After nearly three years of orchestral experience, I am currently the principal cellist of UW-Madison All-University String Orchestra.
